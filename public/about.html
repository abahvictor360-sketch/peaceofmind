Our story

Helping people find calm, clarity, and confidence

For over twelve years our clinicians have supported people through anxiety, burnout, grief and life transitions. We blend evidence-based therapy — CBT, ACT, mindfulness — with warm, human connection.

Today peaceofmind brings that care online and in person, with programs you can follow at your own pace and therapists who genuinely know your story.
