FAQ

Questions, answered

Are your therapists licensed?

Yes. Every clinician is licensed and vetted, with ongoing supervision and training.

Can I switch therapists?

Anytime, at no cost. The right fit matters, and we'll help you find it.

Is my information private?

Absolutely. Sessions are confidential and your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes — cancel or pause from your dashboard in a couple of clicks.

Is this a crisis service?

No. If you are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency number or crisis line.
